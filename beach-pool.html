How It's Built

The Anatomy of a Mediante Beach Pool

A beach entry is more than a sloped floor. Each pool is built up in layers, and every layer is specified for the site's soil and conditions during design.

Layer 01 · Base 01
terrain

Prepared Sub-Base

Excavation, compaction and drainage are prepared to suit the site's soil, so the pool shell sits on stable ground.

Specified per site survey
Layer 02 · Shell 02
view_in_ar

Reinforced Concrete Shell

A continuous reinforced shell forms the beach slope, the swimming area and any built-in seating or ledges.

Detailed in construction drawings
Layer 03 · Barrier 03
water_lock

Waterproofing Layer

A waterproofing system between the shell and the finish protects the structure and keeps the water where it belongs.

Selected for climate and use
Layer 04 · Finish 04
waves

Sand-Finish Surface

The final textured finish gives the shoreline its beach look and a comfortable surface underfoot, without loose sand.

Colour and texture chosen with you

What Matters Standard Tiled Pool Mediante Beach-Entry Pool
Getting In Entry and safety An abrupt drop at the coping edge. Entry is by ladder or steps.

The Finish

The Surface Under Your Feet

The shoreline finish is what makes a beach pool feel like a beach. We choose its colour and texture with you, balancing a natural look, barefoot comfort and easy upkeep.

spa

Barefoot Comfort

A textured but smooth finish made for walking, sitting and lounging right on the slope.

wb_sunny

Cooler Tones

Light sand colours reflect more sunlight than dark tile, so the water's edge stays more comfortable at midday.

cleaning_services

Practical Upkeep

A continuous finish has far fewer joints than tile, which means fewer places for dirt and algae to collect.
